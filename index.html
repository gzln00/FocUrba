The Problem

Winter Roads Kill Cities

Every winter, cities face a triple threat: deadly accidents from ice, toxic salt and chemical damage, and massive public spending on maintenance and lawsuits.

01

Ice Accumulation on Roads

Snow and ice build up on urban roads every winter, creating life-threatening conditions and forcing emergency interventions around the clock.

02

Excessive Winter Maintenance Costs

During the coldest months, cities spend enormously on extended public lighting and reactive maintenance — a cost that compounds an already heavy burden.

03

Salt & Chemicals: Environmental Crisis

Current de-icing relies on highly polluting salts and chemicals — corroding infrastructure, contaminating groundwater, and damaging ecosystems year after year.

04

Accident Costs & Government Compensation

Ice-related accidents generate enormous losses through emergency response, hospitalisation, and compensation claims that drain public budgets continuously.

How It Works

Three Sources. One Smart Road.

Foc Urbà captures energy from multiple sources simultaneously, stores it intelligently, and distributes it as heat directly beneath the asphalt to keep every road above 0°C.

01

Braking Energy Capture

Piezoelectric crystals embedded beneath the asphalt convert the kinetic energy released by braking vehicles into electricity — automatically, with no moving parts.

02

Data Centre Waste Heat

Helsinki's data centres produce enormous waste heat. Foc Urbà recovers this thermal energy and redirects it through a city-level network into the road heating grid.

03

Marine Energy + Storage

Baltic Sea marine energy, combined with intelligent thermal storage, ensures continuous road heating even during low-traffic periods or calm-weather conditions.

Technology

Built Into the Infrastructure

Piezoelectric Conversion

Crystals under the road convert mechanical pressure from every vehicle into electrical energy. No moving parts, no maintenance, zero traffic disruption.

Keeps Roads Above 0°C Continuously

Maintains road surface above freezing at all times — even in the harshest Nordic winters. No manual intervention. No chemical spraying needed.

Intelligent Energy Storage

Surplus energy generated during high-traffic hours is stored and redistributed overnight or during snowfall, ensuring full 24/7 coverage without waste.

Permanent Public Infrastructure

Once installed, the system is permanent — embedded in the city itself, integrated into public infrastructure, and impossible to copy or replicate competitively.
